# FlowSheet – Process Toolkit · V1 scope

**Status:** DRAFT scope lock, built 10 Nov 2026. Waiting for an FFL process engineer to
validate it against plant numbers (plan §4 step 3) before release.

There are seven tools (the V1 hard cap). Each tool ships with its formula sheet, the standard it
cites, and at least 3 worked cases that run in `flutter test`. A failing case fails the build.
Every expected value comes from an independent source, never from running the app's own Dart code.

Other tests: `test/input_errors_test.dart` checks that impossible inputs raise
`ToolInputError`. Examples are an F factor that does not exist, a temperature cross, IF97
region 3, saturation above 350 °C, a level above the tank top, and back pressure ≥ relieving
pressure.

Reference scripts are in `apps/process_toolkit/tool/`: `ref_*.py`, `gen_if97.py` and
`gen_if97_sweep.py`. Run them in a venv with `fluids`, `ht`, `iapws` and `CoolProp`, using `python -I`.

## Research notes

- **IF97 coefficients.** `lib/tools/if97_data.dart` is generated by `tool/gen_if97.py` from the
  `iapws` package tables. Every mantissa (14 digits), every sign and the Table 11 exponents were
  checked against the text of the official IAPWS R7-97(2012) PDF: 34 + 9 + 43 + 10 + 5
  coefficients, all of them found. The verification tables of the release then reproduce to all
  9 printed digits. Region 2 uses the normal n°₁, n°₂ (Table 10), not the metastable-vapour values.
- **IF97 scope.** Region 3 (near-critical: above 350 °C and above the B23 line, e.g. 400 °C at
  more than 300 bar) and region 5 (above 800 °C) are not implemented and are refused. Saturation
  is therefore limited to 350 °C / 165.3 bar a. That covers FFL HP steam, about 105–125 bar,
  well inside it.
- **Laminar limit.** The app uses Re < 2000 (Crane), while `fluids` switches at Re 2040 (Avila
  2011). Between 2000 and 4000 the app uses Colebrook and shows a "transitional" warning. No
  case falls in the 2000–2040 band.
- **API 520 Kv: edition difference.** The 10th edition gives Kv = (1 + 170/Re)^−0.5 (valid for
  Re > 80). The 7th–9th editions used (0.9935 + 2.878/Re^0.5 + 342.75/Re^1.5)^−1. The app uses
  the 10th edition.
- **API 520 Kv: Reynolds-number basis (discrepancy with `fluids`).** API's procedure computes Re
  on the next larger API 526 orifice above the Kv = 1 area, and steps up the orifice if needed.
  `fluids.API520_A_l` uses the preliminary area instead. For Example 5 this gives 3 114 mm² from
  `fluids` against 3 122 mm² from API. The `fluids` figure is 0.24 % smaller, which is
  unconservative. The app follows API and reproduces 3 122 mm². The Re of 4 525 printed in
  Example 5 comes from the SSU form of the equation; the cP form gives 4 632. The app also
  applies Kv to thin liquids, so water comes out 0.013 % larger.
- **Balanced bellows in subcritical gas flow (discrepancy with `fluids`).** API 520 §5.6.3
  sizes balanced valves with the critical equation and the maker's Kb. `fluids.API520_A_g`
  switches to the F₂ equation and drops Kb. In the test case that gives 446 mm² against 581 mm²
  from API's method. The app follows API.
- **Orifice areas.** The app uses API 526 areas in in² × 645.16. The mm² values printed in API
  520 are rounded (e.g. P = 4 116 mm²).
- **NPSHa example.** Pumps & Systems converts with 2.31 ft/psi. Exact ρg differs from that by
  0.06 %, so those two cases use a tolerance of 1e-3.
- **Co-current LMTD.** `ht.LMTD` returns 0 when the co-current outlets are equal. The app treats
  that case as infeasible and raises an error.
- **Torispherical heads.** ASME quotes f and k on the outside diameter. The app (like
  `fluids.TANK`) applies them to the inside diameter. The horizontal torispherical result comes
  from numerical integration and matches `fluids` (Jones' exact formulas) to 1e-8.

## Template limitations met (plant_core not changed)

- There is no pressure-gradient unit, so "Friction ΔP per 100 m" is a pressureDiff output.
- There is no molar-mass quantity, so M is dimensionless with a "kg/kmol" suffix.
- A `ToolCase` cannot expect a `ToolInputError`. Error cases therefore live in a separate test file.
- An input's initial value cannot depend on a choice. Kd therefore has two inputs (gas 0.975,
  liquid 0.65).
- The unit converter needs one hidden NumberInput per quantity (19 inputs), because an input's
  quantity is fixed.

## Open items for the FFL validator

1. Provide 3 real plant cases per tool (tag, inputs, expected result). They will be added as `ToolCase`s.
2. Confirm which API 520 Part I edition FFL works to (the Kv formula differs, see above), and
   that balanced-bellows subcritical sizing should use the critical equation with Kb.
3. Confirm the NPSH margin policy: the app warns below NPSHa/NPSHr = 1.1 (HI 9.6.1 minimum).
   Does FFL require a larger margin or a fixed margin in metres?
4. Confirm the torispherical head data used on FFL drawings: ASME F&D (1.0/0.06), Klöpper or
   Korbbogen, and whether it is referred to OD or ID.
5. Confirm the laminar/transitional treatment (2000/4000) and the roughness presets,
   e.g. whether FFL uses 0.045 mm or a larger value for aged carbon-steel lines.
6. Steam: is region 3 (above 350 °C at above about 165 bar) needed anywhere? Steam sizing for
   relief valves (Napier, KSH) and two-phase relief are not in V1.
7. Standard gas flow: FFL's normal conditions (0 °C or 15 °C) for Nm³/h in the converter.
