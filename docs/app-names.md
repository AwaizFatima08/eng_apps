# HomiLabs Plant Tools — app names

**Status:** LOCKED — approved by Homi 10 Nov 2026 (the Play/trademark search below is
still done per app before its release).

Family: **HomiLabs Plant Tools**. Each app keeps the plan's pattern as its subtitle
("*Discipline* Toolkit") and gets a short, memorable brand name for the launcher and
store title, e.g. "LoopCal – Instrument Toolkit".

| Cycle | Discipline | Name | Store title | Package id | Why this name |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Instrument | **LoopCal** | LoopCal – Instrument Toolkit | `com.homilabs.loopcal` | "Loop" + "Cal(ibration/culator)" — what I&C techs do all day |
| 2 | Lab | **TitraLab** | TitraLab – Lab Toolkit | `com.homilabs.titralab` | Titration is the lab's signature task |
| 3 | HSE | **SafeShift** | SafeShift – HSE Toolkit | `com.homilabs.safeshift` | Safety for every shift; noise/heat/exposure are shift-based |
| 4 | Process | **FlowSheet** | FlowSheet – Process Toolkit | `com.homilabs.flowsheet` | The process engineer's core document |
| 5 | Electrical | **VoltWise** | VoltWise – Electrical Toolkit | `com.homilabs.voltwise` | Cable, motor, solar sizing done wisely |
| 6 | Mechanical | **TorqueMate** | TorqueMate – Mechanical Toolkit | `com.homilabs.torquemate` | Bolt torque is the most-used mechanical calc |
| 7 | Civil | **BeamBook** | BeamBook – Civil Toolkit | `com.homilabs.beambook` | Beams + quantities, a site engineer's notebook |

Alternatives if a name is taken on Play: LoopMate, TitraMate, ShiftSafe, PlantFlow,
VoltCalc, ShaftMate, SiteBook.

Before locking each name: search Google Play and the trademark database (IPO Pakistan,
USPTO TESS) for the exact name in the same category.
